## Literature checks that change the interpretation

The text subtotal in the pinned Qwen config can be reproduced from `d=5120`, `f=17408`, vocabulary `V=248320`, full-attention query and KV widths `q=6144` and `k=1024`, and linear key/value widths `u=2048` and `v=6144`. Count `2dV` for the input and output matrices, `64×3df` for SwiGLU, `16d(3q+2k)` for gated full-attention projections, and `48d(2u+3v+2×48)` for the linear-attention projections. The terms are 2,542,796,800, 17,112,760,320, 1,677,721,600 and 5,560,074,240, respectively. Add small norm and convolution tensors for a full text-only count. A release-level slot count includes vision and MTP as well. The formula uses the pinned configuration and indexed tensor families. [Config](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3.8-27B/resolve/37561816e66b5b9153b1b64c10968c18c1e6f4a0/config.json) · [Tensor index](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3.8-27B/resolve/37561816e66b5b9153b1b64c10968c18c1e6f4a0/model.safetensors.index.json)

- The iso-depth study fits a validation-loss exponent of 0.46 over 1, 2, 4 and 8 applications and reports 0.65 with hyperconnections in a separate case study. The fit does not predict factual recall or validate the 25-pass case. Ouro separates manipulation gains from storage in its controls. Its abstract gives no numerical universal storage cap. [Iso-depth v4](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.21106v4) · [Ouro v6](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.25741v6)
- Engram reports Big-Bench Hard and MATH gains over a matched-parameter, matched-FLOP MoE control and explores deterministic addressing that supports host prefetch. That comparison does not establish stored-byte advantage against a native low-bit dense control, and transfer from host violates the proposed deployment constraint. [Engram](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.07372)
- LC-QAT trains a code representation after a strong post-training initialization. MXCode studies approximately two-bit vector quantization with MXFP4-compatible inference. Both constrain novelty and supply useful deployment controls. Neither establishes an advantage for native training from random initialization at equal measured resident bytes. [LC-QAT](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v306/wang26kc.html) · [MXCode](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.00403v2)
- Byte Latent Transformer uses local byte models and patching to change the vocabulary/state trade. It does not eliminate embeddings or output costs entirely and the patch representations have their own storage and compute. [BLT](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.09871)
