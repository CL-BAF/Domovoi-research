## Question and hypothesis

How much held-out loss can eight independently parameterized blocks recover at the shared token budget? Greater capacity could improve later loss, while larger unique state predicts additional optimizer and storage cost.

## Interpretation and limitations

The measured early loss and resources belong to a short infrastructure check. The early ranking can change at longer budgets and with another LR horizon. No held-out test or 8K inference qualification was performed. [Campaign analysis](../TINYSTORIES-CALIBRATION-2026-11-24.md)
