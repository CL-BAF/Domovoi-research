# Hypothesis and findings ledger

Dated evidence snapshot 24 November 2026. “Supported” refers to the described scope. These descriptions are designed to be updated by dated additions.

| ID · area | Question and prior hypothesis | Supporting evidence and implementation | Observations and interpretation | Confidence, alternatives, confounders | Status and verification |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| H-01 · Stored tensors | Does depth tying reduce trainable state at fixed applied depth? Predicted yes. | DML-0005–0007 and DML-0008–0010, d914684. Architecture parameter derivation and object-identity tests. | R02×4 stores 10,665,856 vs B08 33,812,224. AdamW serialization differs by about 176.65 MiB. The stored-block accounting predicts the direction. | High for this architecture arithmetic and observed files. Extra activation and optimizer workspace effects remain. | **Supported**. Independently profile tagged allocations, and check future alternative implementations. |
| H-02 · Peak training resources | Does R02×4 reduce peak allocated memory at 512 vs B08? Predicted yes. | Both calibration rounds and extended-v1; f5fac7a and d914684. | 406.33 MiB or 18.00% less. Also 939.60 MiB more than B02. Tying does not make applied depth cheap. | High for these measured allocator peaks. Other GPU allocators, schedules and input geometry may differ. | **Supported at tested geometry**. Re-measure at long contexts and inspect allocator traces. |
| H-03 · Runtime | Does lower stored state accelerate equal-depth training? Direction plausible despite equal modeled matmul work. | RTX smoke in both orders, calibration V2 and extended-v1. | R02×4 was 9.72% faster in deterministic calibration and about 9.71% faster in extended synchronized step rate. | Moderate for same machine. Other workloads, CUDA clocks, kernels and optimizer traffic can explain part of it. | **Supported locally**. Balanced-order repeats and kernel profiling would distinguish mechanisms. |
| H-04 · Early quality | Does additional applied depth beat B02 immediately under the short 200-update schedule? An early win was possible. | DML-0005–0007, deterministic calibration. | B02 leads R02×4 by 0.17258 and B08 by 0.12709 nats/target. | High for observed single seed. Warmup length, initialization scales, corpus, seed variance and gradient behavior confound causal interpretation. | **Contradicted at this calibration budget**. Run preregistered, paired seeds/schedule ablation after publication. |
| H-05 · Longer-budget trade | Can tied depth regain some of the deeper model's benefit at equal tokens? Hypothesis partial gain. | DML-0008–0010 at matched 610, 1,526, 3,052 and 6,104 steps. | By 100M R02×4 is 0.05731 nats better than B02 and 0.14319 behind B08. About 28.58% gap closure. | Conditional to one seed and TinyStories. Fewer independent weights and tied gradient alignment may contribute. No test or broader corpus score. | **Supported descriptively, generalization inconclusive**. Freeze decision and replicate seeds; assess time-matched quality and held-out test. |
| H-06 · Recovery | Does deterministic mode remove observed same-device replay drift? | RCV-DEFAULT, RCV-DET-MANUAL and RCV-DET-PINNED; f5fac7a/d914684. | 0.001584119 vs 0.0 max final absolute parameter delta. State restore was exact in both. | High for the bounded control, low for other devices or extended exactness. Kernel attribution untested. | **Supported for tested control**. Expand interruption points and compare supported hardware/driver combinations. |
| H-07 · Final inference objective | Can a capable variant qualify at ≤4 GiB total, 8K, batch 1, ≥8 tok/s, no offload? | Only logical byte equations and a CUDA profiler smoke exist. | Equal application depth leaves logical KV unchanged by tying. Existing learned table holds 512 positions. | No empirical qualification. Cache backing, workspaces and device usage dominate near a hard ceiling. | **Open**. See [inference qualification plan](../architecture/memory-complexity.md). |
| H-08 · Sequence order | Could sequence ordering improve tied computation or training stability? | CPU recurrence order correctness only. | Both orders unroll the intended schedule. Cycle was the only trained order. | Quality and resource differences unknown. | **Proposed**. Balanced, otherwise matched ordering ablation on frozen data. |
