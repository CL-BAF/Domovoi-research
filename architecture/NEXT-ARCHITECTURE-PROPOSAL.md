# Domovoi architecture directions

24 November 2026 · planning proposal only. The hypotheses and thresholds here are candidates for review. They will require separate implementation commits and approved experiment records before any execution. No protocol ID has been reserved.

## Research objective and evidence

Find a design that improves capability per GiB, learning at a fixed token and FLOP budget, context-memory efficiency, useful computation per parameter, and inference speed. The hardware qualification target remains **4 GiB total GPU inference memory**, **8K usable context**, and **at least 8 output tokens/s** at batch 1 with no CPU or disk model offload. Use the RTX 5080 for reference measurements, with affinity and driver recorded.

On TinyStories, R02×4 demonstrates a training resource and quality trade at equal token count. It recovered 28.6% of B08's validation-loss gain over B02 in the extended run and used 18.0% less peak training allocation than B08. The one paired seed, short trained context, corpus scope and training-only memory measurements leave general capability, usable 8K context and deployment fit open. Keep B02, B08 and R02×4 as historical controls where the budget and context are comparable. Add a fresh full-attention Transformer and a fixed-depth lightweight local-attention control at each new scale. [Extended campaign](../experiments/TINYSTORIES-EXTENDED-2026-11-24.md)

## Competing mechanisms

### A. Selective state with sparse exact attention

Use a selective recurrent state in most layers and a small number of local GQA layers for exact nearby binding. A selective state-space layer can be abstracted as $S_t=A(x_t) S_{t-1}+B(x_t)x_t^\top$ with a learned readout. Mamba-2 establishes prior art for structured state-space duality and efficient selective layers. The Domovoi hypothesis is that alternating state-space mixing with a sparse allocation of exact attention can meet the memory budget while retaining part of the full-attention model's long-distance reasoning performance. This is a hypothesis about the allocation of exact attention and retained state. [Mamba-2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.21060)

An illustrative BF16 state with 16 blocks, width 1,024 and 64 state values per channel is **2 MiB**. Four local GQA blocks with 512 retained tokens, four KV heads and 128 dimensions per head need **4 MiB** of logical KV. These are lower-level storage estimates. Include convolution buffers, workspaces, weights and allocator costs in deployment measurements. State compression may lose rare names or distant links, and reference kernels may be slow at batch 1.

Compare state-only, fixed local attention, and hybrid variants with matched weights, training targets and estimated FLOPs. At 8K the hybrid must improve multi-hop retrieval by at least five percentage points over state-only and keep 2K validation NLL within 0.05 nats/target of full attention at the same tokenizer. Report performance by retrieval distance and number of distractors. Drop the allocation rule if either gate fails.

### B. Token-adaptive computation with a shared latent history

Route a bounded number of extra applications to tokens whose prospective prediction uncertainty or retrieval demand warrants it. Store a fixed-width causal latent per historical token, with per-application read projections. Mixture-of-Depths demonstrates fixed-budget token-wise computation routing. The Domovoi hypothesis is that coupling causal routing to a shared cache representation can retain the reasoning benefit of deeper computation at lower *realized* per-token work. Router inputs must be available at that token in prefill and decode. Compare a learned router with uniform depth and equal-budget random routing. [Mixture-of-Depths](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.02258)

With 16 layers and one BF16 latent of width 128 per layer and token, logical history at 8,192 tokens is **32 MiB**. Ordinary BF16 KV with four 128-dimensional heads at the same layer count is **256 MiB**. The latent estimate assumes on-demand projection without persistent expanded KV. A mean of 1.5 passes versus two fixed passes removes 25% of block applications before router and projection overhead. The quality gate is within 0.02 nats/target of fixed depth at matched training tokens, improved reasoning or retrieval relative to equal-budget random routing, and at least 10% faster median batch-1 decode. Failed cache parity, per-example work caps or tail latency can disqualify the design even if the means improve.

### C. Learn when to retain exact facts and when to consolidate

Keep a 256-token exact recent window and allocate a 128-slot bounded memory for the rest. A learned writer chooses which summaries or individual spans to preserve. Use fixed-size slots with age and position metadata so the read path is causal and budgeted. Titans is prior art for learned long-term memory, surprise-sensitive updates, and combining memory with attention. The Domovoi hypothesis is that training an explicit retention-versus-summary decision on delayed factual queries will improve retention over uniform compression at the same memory and update budget. [Titans](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.00663)

At 16 layers, four KV heads, width 128 and BF16, treating the 384 retained positions as ordinary KV gives **12 MiB** of logical storage. Asynchronous writes, temporary consolidation activations and slot metadata add cost. Frequent updates may stall decode, and a writer that predicts the wrong queries can erase facts permanently. Compare recency-only, uniform pooling, learned consolidation and a same-budget exact retrieval control. Preregister a minimum five-point 8K multi-hop gain over pooling with fewer than two points of short-context loss. Use distractors, conflicting updates and changed query timing to detect shortcuts.

### D. Bounded procedural state for compositional reasoning

Let a compact controller emit typed, task-local operations over a fixed-size latent scratch state. Replay operations to reconstruct intermediate values and enforce a hard step budget. DreamCoder introduces a learned reusable symbolic library and program induction. The Domovoi hypothesis is that a small reusable operation set will generalize to unseen compositions within a tight compute and memory window, while preserving language behavior through a conventional readout. The value would be transfer beyond predefined templates. [DreamCoder](https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.08381)

A 64-slot state of width 512 costs **64 KiB** in BF16 per active controller instance before weights, readouts and working memory. Cap the controller at 16 operations per generated token and measure dispatch overhead and p95 time explicitly. Compare a fixed-depth dense controller, a same-budget recurrent latent controller without typed operations, and the proposed controller on held-out operators, graph sizes and compositions. Require a ten-point exact-match gain on held-out composition with at most two points loss on matched in-distribution examples and latency within the decode target. Discrete credit assignment, policy collapse and templates bleeding into test are major risks.

## Staging and costs

Start with A and a matched local-attention control because state and KV sizes can be instrumented independently. B tests a separate hypothesis about conditional compute. C has a clean same-budget pooling comparison. D should enter the queue only after a frozen composition benchmark defines meaningful held-out cases. Preserve negative outcomes and analyse one experiment before starting another.

A first screen could use 100–300M parameters and 25M training targets per arm, with at least three paired seeds and matched data order. For conventional dense layers, $6P T$ gives **about 15–45 PFLOP per arm per seed** at those sizes. Use actual parameter applications and state or attention work for routed and recurrent variants. Publish both fixed-token and fixed-FLOP comparisons. Estimate real wall time from separately approved profiling evidence before committing a deadline. The first screen should establish a measurable learning or memory advantage before any 8K-scale training. A separate study would test whether the advantage transfers beyond TinyStories to broader frozen and decontaminated data.

Use a candidate deployment envelope of **2.0 GiB weights and scales**, **0.5 GiB persistent state/cache**, **1.0 GiB temporary workspaces and runtime**, and **0.5 GiB margin**. These are budget allocations. An illustrative 1.5B weights at four bits occupy about 0.70 GiB before scales, alignment and runtime copies. The allocator and device peaks must be measured from cold load through prefill and decode. Record absolute peak GPU use on an otherwise idle GPU and independently reconcile process, reserved and allocated bytes. Verify that weights and model state remain on device. Document any software runtime allocations on the host.

## Qualification and capability reference

- **Useful 8K.** Use 8,192 prompt tokens under the tested tokenizer plus at least 256 generated tokens within the engine's supported context, with no truncation. Run short and 8K RULER retrieval, aggregation and multi-hop configurations on frozen unseen seeds. Add a natural-document delayed-evidence set. Freeze a proposed qualification floor of 80% aggregate and no more than a ten-point drop from the 2K reference, with per-task exact match and intervals. Protocol approval must review the floor and configuration before scoring. [RULER](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.06654)
- **Speed and total memory.** Measure batch-1 steady decode and report prefill, time to first token, p95 token latency, peak device memory and maximum resident state. Use three fresh launches per condition at 8K, synchronized and with at least 256 output tokens per timed sequence. Require at least 8 output tokens/s in each repeat and a total peak no greater than 4,096 MiB. Treat OOM, truncation, hidden offload and unmatched workload as failures. Record tokenizer differences and report output bytes/s as a supplementary comparison.
- **27B capability comparison.** Use Google DeepMind's `google/gemma-3-27b-it` at Hugging Face revision `005ad3404e59d6023443cb575daa05336842228a` as the text capability reference, with released instruction template and documented BF16 or versioned quantized comparison conditions on fit-capable hardware. Its quality is a separate comparison from Domovoi's local hardware qualification. Freeze dataset and harness revisions, prompts, decoding, example IDs, exclusions and metrics. Use ARC-Challenge for choice reasoning, GSM8K exact match for multi-step arithmetic, strict IFEval for instruction following, and RULER for contextual work. Include contamination screening, confidence intervals by task and per-task failures. Use matching raw examples and document model-specific token and chat formatting. Compare comparable instruction-tuned checkpoints, or state the training-regime mismatch. Single aggregate parity cannot establish broad 27B-class capability. [Gemma 3 technical report](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.19786) · [Pinned reference](https://huggingface.co/google/gemma-3-27b-it/tree/005ad3404e59d6023443cb575daa05336842228a) · [ARC](https://arxiv.org/abs/1803.05457) · [GSM8K](https://arxiv.org/abs/2110.14168) · [IFEval](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.07911)

The next decision is which mechanism gets a separately reviewed plan, cost estimate and stop criteria. This proposal records competing directions and evaluation choices.
