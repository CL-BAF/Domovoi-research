# Phased Domovoi architecture research roadmap

24 November 2026 · all phases proposed. The goal is a genuinely better native neural architecture at ≤4 GiB total GPU inference, batch one, useful 8K and ≥8 generated tokens/s, with broad 27B-class text capability as the long-term target. Research stages below may find a useful narrower result or disprove a direction. Approval of this roadmap alone authorizes no workload. [Reconciliation](RESEARCH-RECONCILIATION-2026-11-24.md) · [Qualification draft](../methodology/27B-4GIB-QUALIFICATION-SPECIFICATION.md)

| 2 · Transfer and a second size | Train surviving C2 against the strongest C0 at approximately 10M, 30M and 100M logical scale, with frozen byte targets and three paired seeds as a first natural-text screen. Start with 20M common processed tokens per arm, shared tokenizer, disjoint documents, and separately matched-compute endpoints. Retain narrow synthetic conclusions. | RTX 5080 for early scales, under separately approved disk/data prep. About 5.6–56 modeled GPU-hours at 5–0.5 TFLOP/s before code overhead. Existing FineWeb-Edu preview has 5,044,464 train tokens and cannot supply an untouched 20M-token stream. Verify raw access, license, versions and packing first. |

Model rooflines using named measured transfer and codec counters, then verify actual speed. | Stop a kernel line if recompute or unsupported instructions miss the physical rate budget. Reassess compression rate or choose C0. | RTX 5080 first for instrumentation and 4 GiB device for qualification. Size workload as output tokens × required repetitions plus cold load/preprocessing. | 4 · Factored compute and association | Use C0 or surviving C2 weight representation across C0, memory-only, loop-only and re-query C1 at equal resident bytes. Promote context mixer only if a measured context bottleneck appears. | Early ~10–60M scale on RTX 5080 subject to a separate plan. Model training ops from actual applied layers, key scoring, sparse reads and computation per latent step, not total unique parameters alone. Confirm p95 on the named 4 GiB platform. |

Include teacher output integrity, cutoff and contamination checks. | Architecture main effect survives without the teacher or a clearly disclosed interaction justifies its own result. | Stop architecture claim if only teacher changes explain gain. Keep/distinguish a training-recipe result. | Small approved teacher may fit 5080. Qwen BF16 and larger corpora need extra hardware. | 6 · Scale confirmation | Advance only surviving mechanisms on at least three measured byte targets and replicate just before extrapolating. Validate a resident-byte/quality slope, actual physical inference and held-out task generalization. | Lower uncertainty bounds on quality-per-byte exceed strong C0, including generalization and speed checks. | Stop if the advantage shrinks beyond uncertainty, violates the physical budget, or requires offload. Avoid extrapolating 27B capability from partial benchmark gains or reference-suite overfitting. | Physical 4 GiB GPU for candidate plus fit-capable offline GPU(s) for the references. Need approved datasets, harness, resources and dependencies. |

## Optional capability ladder

Separately propose a read-only capability study on a pinned small-model family ladder, preferably Qwen3.5 0.8B, 2B, 4B and 9B plus pinned Qwen3.8-27B. Add Gemma 3 27B IT for family sensitivity. Each version, native and optional post-training compressed variant, family change and bytes must be marked. Partition the benchmark battery into clean factual recall, mixed general knowledge and label-free procedural reasoning. The curves may show whether benchmark-level performance needs more stored knowledge than a strong C0 can supply. They do not estimate the actual mutual information in the Qwen weights. This can move C1 ahead of further C2 work if factual accuracy saturates early and reasoning remains weak. Running these models requires separate approval, weights access, pinned datasets/harness and fit-capable offline resources. [Reference policy](../methodology/27B-4GIB-QUALIFICATION-SPECIFICATION.md)

## Conditional mechanisms

- If full-context cache sets the limit, compare fixed local/global attention and a selective-state hybrid under identical token data and resident bytes. Test exact distant recall before testing learned consolidation. Keep both the existing efficient hybrid and full attention as comparators.
- If p95 inference work is the limit, compare fixed computation, learned routing and equal-budget random routing. Count gating and any latent-cache expansion before drawing speed conclusions. Test MTP separately with matched accepted-token quality and charge both runtime and draft storage.
- If C1 is convincingly specific on procedural failure modes, a typed or routed program can be a later experiment with a held-out composition suite and lesion control. A conventional dense or low-rank control hidden within a larger external system does not count as a new neural mechanism.

## Protocol and approval order

Research documentation and source checks can proceed now. For experiments, document mechanism, hypothesis, predicted and alternative outcomes, metrics, control configurations, required resources, stop criteria, source and asset SHAs, commands and provenance. Update README and index, push the plan, get human approval, and satisfy the remote launch gate. After every outcome, including failure, retain raw or receipted data and publish analysis and interpretation before another experiment. Do not use old checkouts or direct APIs as alternate research launchers. The test runner, transfer corpora, exact GPU, kernel feasibility and score pins are implementation or approval dependencies.
