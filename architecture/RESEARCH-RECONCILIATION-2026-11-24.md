# Reconciling the Domovoi architecture investigations

24 November 2026 · Codex synthesis for human review. The original [GitHub proposal](NEXT-ARCHITECTURE-PROPOSAL.md) and [independent Claude investigation](../reviews/2026-11-24-independent-architecture-research.md) remain separate records. All decisions and execution proposals here are pending.

## Decision

Build the study around a competitive **C0 native scalar low-bit control**. Give the first research comparison to **C2 native coded weights**, conditional on a deployed path and an equal-rate scalar control. Keep **C1 GPU-resident factored memory and a small looped core** as the next independent mechanism if a measured byte-read bottleneck or multi-hop deficit survives C0. Use selective state and local attention as context controls. Defer adaptive routing, consolidation, and procedural libraries until their specific bottlenecks are measured. This is an ordering of questions to test, with a different evidence bar for committing to an architecture.

## Evidence against starting with C2

The strongest objection is the combination of representation loss and runtime overhead. In the controlled tuple study, INT8 post-training quantization preserves the reported roughly two bits of knowledge per parameter, while the tested INT4 GPTQ condition averaged about 0.7 bits per parameter. That reduces its information per stored bit even before adding scales. Native ternary and the fitted precision laws show that training in a low-bit representation is viable, with a precision, data, and capacity trade. They supply no evidence that native vector codes beat a tuned scalar control in factual retrieval per device byte. LC-QAT starts with a strong initialized quantized model. MXCode addresses vector reconstruction on native FP4 hardware. Neither is evidence for the proposed from-scratch mechanism on an RTX 2050. [Tuple study](https://arxiv.org/html/2404.05405) · [Scaling Laws for Precision](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.04330) · [LC-QAT](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v306/wang26kc.html) · [MXCode](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.00403v2)

The strongest positive case is a discriminating, relatively inexpensive experiment. C0 and C2 can share data, backbone, optimization budget and packing metadata while testing code structure directly. If scalar quantization wins at equal stored bytes and compute, there is no reason to scale the tested C2 design. If learned codes tie fixed codes, the measured effect is attributable to the rate or representation family so far, and a learned dictionary claim is unsupported. If synthetic retrieval improves and real-language modeling does not, retain the synthetic result and stop deployment scaling.

## What the evidence still leaves open

- How much benchmark-relevant factual information Qwen actually uses under a matched reasoning budget. The 53.8 Gbit estimate applies a synthetic empirical density to a much larger unrelated model.
- Whether a codebook can exceed a strong native scalar model after counting every scale, codebook and buffer and fixing the decoder workload. The resident footprint and memory traffic may move in different directions.
- Whether native learned codes sustain their gain beyond random table recall, held-out phrasing and a tiny model. Structured natural knowledge has a different conditional entropy.
- Whether retrieval and recomputation trade in C1 is favorable after the output head, irregular memory reads, cache misses and tail latency are included on an Ampere 4 GiB device.
- Which physical 4 GiB hardware is available, which kernels it supports, and how much driver/display headroom is genuinely usable.
