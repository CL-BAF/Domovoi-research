# AI efficiency evidence matrix

24 November 2026 · literature and accounting for the roadmap. **Measured** refers to the named paper's setup or the linked Domovoi record. **Derived** follows from a stated format or workload. **Open** requires a new test. Results from other models, runtimes or devices cannot be inserted into Domovoi's measured results.

## Weight representation and training

| Mechanism and prior art | What the evidence supports | Cost and deployment consequence |
|---|---|---|
| BF16 and FP16 | Both use 16 stored bits for a dense value before metadata. BF16 reserves more exponent bits and FP16 more fractional precision. Geometry and implementation can dominate training stability. | An approximately 26.89B text subtotal needs 50.09 GiB at either format. Training commonly also has FP32 optimizer slots and sometimes master weights. Retain BF16 reference and report inference and training formats separately. [BF16 study](https://arxiv.org/abs/1905.12322) · [Mixed precision](https://arxiv.org/abs/1710.03740) |
| INT8 and INT4 scalar PTQ, GPTQ and AWQ | GPTQ reported approximately 3–4 bit reconstruction on studied large models. AWQ uses activation-aware scaling and 4-bit kernels. Reported speedups depend on kernels and hardware. The tuple study found INT8 competitive and INT4 GPTQ worse on factual-bit capacity in its setup. | Native format needs scale/zero point/outlier fees. BF16 group scales at 64 weights add 0.25 bits/weight. INT4 can be slower when unpack and conversion dominate. PTQ is a deployment diagnostic distinct from the native-fit track. [GPTQ](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.17323) · [AWQ](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.00978) · [Tuple study](https://arxiv.org/html/2404.05405) |
| Scalar QAT and precision scaling | QAT trains with quantization effects and can recover useful accuracy. Precision laws fit effective capacity and loss for measured weight, activation and cache configurations. Architecture, data amount, saturation and task sensitivity remain relevant. | Keep floating-point proxy/optimizer during training as desired, but export only the learned compact path. Count quantizer search, clipping and gradient work. Compare factual recall and exact-answer reasoning as well as NLL. [Scaling Laws for Precision](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.04330) |
| Binary, ternary and mixed precision | BitNet and Spectra train low-bit language models. BitNet b1.58 2B4T reports comparable selected benchmarks to similar-size dense models after 4T training tokens plus implementation-specific efficiency gains. | Binary indices have one bit; ideal equally used ternary symbols carry `log2(3)`; practical random-access packing may use two bits. Mixed-precision embedding, norm, scales and activations raise overall rate. The 2B results do not establish a 27B capability or native GPU speed on a 2050. [BitNet](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.11453) · [b1.58](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.17764) · [2B4T](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.12285) · [Spectra](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.12327) |
| Vector and additive quantization | AQLM and QuIP# use codebooks/lattice structure in extreme compression of pretrained LLMs. QTIP uses trellis constraints and asks how decoding can stay efficient. They show low nominal rates with retained task accuracy in measured regimes. | Group size, number of indices, book sizes, scale values, rotations, layout and padding determine effective bits. Decode may be bandwidth- or reconstruction-limited. Competing deployed measurements must include expansion and transient duplicates. [AQLM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.06118) · [QuIP#](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.04396) · [QTIP](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.11235) |
| Learned codebooks and VQ-QAT | DKM differentiably clusters for compression. LC-QAT learns a vector representation using PTQ initialization. MXCode studies approximately two-bit VQ compatible with MXFP4. | All are close precedents. Cold-start assignment stability, reconstruction on Ampere and conditioned factual bits per resident byte remain open. Match intensity of hyperparameter search and count both optimizer and decode work. [DKM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2108.12659) · [LC-QAT](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v306/wang26kc.html) · [MXCode](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.00403v2) |
| Learned dictionaries, factorized or low-rank weights | Shared factors and low-rank replacements exploit correlations. SVD-LLM shows recovery techniques for truncated factors. LoRA shows parameter-efficient adaptation of a frozen backbone. | For dense `m × n` matrix, two rank-`r` factors store `r(m+n)` values and use two matvecs. Reduction requires `r(m+n) < mn` plus viable kernels. LoRA's frozen backbone still counts. Rank can eliminate rare directions. [SVD-LLM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.07378) · [LoRA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.09685) |
| Unstructured, block or N:M sparsity | SparseGPT demonstrates one-shot sparsification on studied pretrained models. Block or N:M formats trade flexibility for reduced indexing and supported instructions. | Sparse bytes include nonzeros, indices/masks and alignment. Speed requires actual supported kernels and compatible dimensions on the target card. Sparse activity in an MoE is distinct from reducing total stored weights. [SparseGPT](https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.00774) · [NVIDIA cuSPARSELt documentation](https://docs.nvidia.com/cuda/cusparselt/) |
| Entropy coding and reconstructed weights | Han et al. combine quantization/pruning and Huffman coding. Entropy-constrained training demonstrates differentiable incentives for compressible weights in studied networks. | Smaller files give no GPU savings if fully expanded at load. Blockwise stream decoding needs offsets, restart state and random access plus scratch. Include decoder state, temporary higher-precision tile, both live representations and decompression instructions in the roofline. [Deep Compression](https://arxiv.org/abs/1510.00149) · [Entropy-constrained training](https://arxiv.org/abs/1812.07520) |
| Embedding, output head, and tokenization | Tying shares a matrix used in input and output. Byte Latent Transformer uses patches to distribute byte-level compute. The pinned Qwen model has separate input and output weights, totaling 2.543B slots. | Shared weights still perform the full output projection. At 65,536 × 2,048, tied INT8 head/input alone is 128 MiB before scales and reads. Bytes or alternative tokens can increase sequence length, tokenizer differences distort raw NLL and context length, and hierarchical heads change accuracy and latency. [Weight tying](https://arxiv.org/abs/1608.05859) · [BLT](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.09871) · [Qwen config](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3.8-27B/resolve/37561816e66b5b9153b1b64c10968c18c1e6f4a0/config.json) |
| Knowledge density and data curation | Allen-Zhu and Li recover about 2 bits/parameter in several well-exposed synthetic tuple regimes and find exposure and junk mixture matter. Morris et al. estimate approximately 3.6 bits/parameter for a different random-data memorization quantity. | There is no demonstrated constant translating these metrics into used 27B facts. Conditional entropy depends on data structure and available side information. Optimizer state or dictionaries that encode evaluation labels must never sit uncounted in a deployment. [Tuple capacity](https://arxiv.org/html/2404.05405) · [Memorization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.24832) |
| Distillation | Teacher can change sample efficiency and reachable behavior at a fixed student size. Distillation scaling shows dependence on teacher capacity and data. | Keep teacher identical across arms, bill teacher tokens/FLOPs, and separate from the architecture main effect. Cross-tokenizer sequence distillation and shared-tokenizer logit distillation have different information paths. Teacher is absent at qualification. [Distillation Scaling Laws](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.08606) |

## Runtime, reasoning, and context

| Mechanism and prior art | Measured or supported effect | Domovoi consequence |
|---|---|---|
| SSM, linear attention and hybrids | Mamba-2's paper reports 2–8× faster core layers in tested regimes. Gated DeltaNet is used by the pinned Qwen family. | Sequence state can be constant in length for selected layers and local attention bounded. Losing exact long-distance binding, recurrent state dtype and unfused kernels may erase gains. Leading reference already uses this class. [Mamba-2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.21060) · [Gated DeltaNet](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.06464) |
| Tied depth and latent loops | Iso-depth fit finds `r^0.46` validation-loss equivalence across one to eight applications. Ouro reports knowledge manipulation gains separate from capacity. Domovoi R02×4 recovers 28.6% of the B08 validation-loss gain over B02 at equal tokens. | Weight reuse can improve stored-weight efficiency while increasing compute and per-depth state. A small repeated core changes the bandwidth equation. A fitted exponent is neither a hardware speedup nor an extrapolation to large `r`. [Iso-depth](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.21106v4) · [Ouro](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.25741v6) · [Domovoi extended run](../experiments/TINYSTORIES-EXTENDED-2026-11-24.md) |
| Sparse associative memory and experts | PKM reads a small set of learned values. Engram reports BBH +5.0 and MATH +2.4 points over its iso-parameter/iso-FLOP MoE comparator. PEER and Switch demonstrate conditional computation at scale. | All learned values, keys and experts still occupy GPU memory. Sparse and indirect access can cost more than the aggregate bytes indicate. Multi-hop read reuse, routing and memory benefit each need their own ablation. [PKM](https://arxiv.org/abs/1907.05242) · [Engram](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.07372) · [PEER](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.04153) · [Switch](https://arxiv.org/abs/2101.03961) |
| Adaptive depth and latent caching | Mixture-of-Depths and Mixture-of-Recursions use selective extra work rather than uniform execution. | Savings are in scheduled operations. At batch 1 a gate, divergent data movement and projection of compressed cache may increase p95 time. Require exact causal parity and compare learned with equal-budget random routing. [MoD](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.02258) · [MoR](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.10524) |
| Learned consolidation and test-time memory | Titans and ATLAS learn context updates and retrieval. | Useful 8K quality can improve per retained byte, with write cost and failure on rare identifiers. Count temporal state, updates, parameters and transient work. A learned contextual store is distinct from a resident factual dictionary. [Titans](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.00663) · [ATLAS](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.23735) |
| Multi-token prediction and speculation | Gloeckle et al. report up to roughly 3× speedup in tested setups using multi-token prediction. | Count draft representations, verification, rejects and synchronization. Use accepted tokens per cycle and actual bytes/FLOPs. Never credit E2E speed from an assumed acceptance multiplier. [MTP](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.19737) |
| Procedural modules | DreamCoder learns a symbolic library. NPI and modular networks organize computations. Controlled studies show architectural modularity alone can be insufficient for systematic composition. | Test typed or neural operators with selective ablation and equal budget. Include whole library, router and calls. The mechanism does not by itself establish factual capacity or general language reasoning. [DreamCoder](https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.08381) · [NPI](https://arxiv.org/abs/1511.06279) · [Modularity study](https://arxiv.org/abs/2206.02713) |

## Accounting conventions

Let `P` be dense weights. A fixed-width stored tensor uses `P b/8` bytes. For vector size `g`, `m` code indices of `b_c` bits per vector, FP16 scale per `G` weights, and `S) copies of `m) BF16 codebooks, the idealized deployed representation is `ceil(P/g) m b_c / 8 + 2 ceil(P/G) + 2 S m 2^b_c g` bytes before alignment. Separately record immutable parameters, exact packed buffers, resident GPU bytes, peak total physical bytes, and bytes read per output token. Count runtime decoder state and all shipped alternatives. Exclude training optimizer state from inference, log it separately.

For the same workload, attainable tokens/s is bounded by the smallest of `BW_eff / bytes_read_per_token`, `F_eff / FLOPs_per_token`, and the reciprocal of critical-path launch and synchronization time. Work overlaps selectively, and effective ceilings need measurement. Compression helps bandwidth only if it reduces traffic across the relevant memory tier and the decode path can use it without expanding the whole model. Even small sparse reads can bottleneck on coalescing or lookup latency.
