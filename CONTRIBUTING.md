# Contributing to Domovoi Research

This repository is the authoritative record of plans, measurements, interpretations and findings. [Domovoi Labs](https://github.com/CL-BAF/Domovoi-Labs) is the authoritative source for code. Link the exact source commit in each new research plan.

Publish the approved plan before starting an experiment. Publish the complete outcome and interpretation before starting the next experiment, including after failure or interruption. Follow the [experiment protocol](docs/EXPERIMENT_PROTOCOL.md) and keep the README and [archive index](experiments/ARCHIVE-INDEX.md) current.

Keep earlier records and superseded evidence accessible. Distinguish measured facts, derived quantities and explanations. Publish portable, appropriately sized evidence and provide provenance and digests for restricted or large artifacts. Keep private paths and credentials out of records and execution notes.

The canonical experiment ledger lives on `research/real-data-calibration`. Use fast-forward updates and review changes to the ledger and plans. Keep the ref available across PR review and merge. Use a separate review branch for longer edits, then integrate its reviewed records into the canonical ref before execution.
