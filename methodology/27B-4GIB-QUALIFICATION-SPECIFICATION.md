# Draft 27B capability and 4 GiB deployment specification

24 November 2026 · **S1–S7 pending human approval.** The target is broad text capability approaching a strong 27B model, in at most 4,096 MiB total device memory, with batch size one, at least 8,192 usable context tokens, and at least eight generated tokens/s. A qualifying model performs inference locally on the GPU with its canonical native representation. Model weights, learned stores and computation stay on-device, with no CPU/disk offload, no retrieval service, no external model and no tool use. Host tokenization and the independent test harness are allowed. Training hardware is separate. The target remains ambitious and unverified. The existing [implementation specification](https://github.com/CL-BAF/Domovoi-Labs/blob/8252fbf361b387dc21d17fd6ff7443d9dc7274ad/RESEARCH.md) and [evaluation definitions](https://github.com/CL-BAF/Domovoi-Labs/blob/8252fbf361b387dc21d17fd6ff7443d9dc7274ad/EVALS.md) remain the historical source.

## S1 · Reference GPU

**Recommendation.** Qualify on a named physical 4 GiB GPU. Prefer the historical RTX 2050 Laptop GPU if available and reproducible. Record the actual SKU and reported usable memory, PCI device identifier, VBIOS, OS, driver, CUDA version, clock/power limits and display configuration. Run RTX 5080 experiments as development and software-budget screens. Its 960 GB/s and CUDA capability 12.0 differ from the Ampere-class 2050 and its nominal 112 GB/s memory scenario. At the same assumed efficiency that is a theoretical 8.57× bandwidth ratio. Verify the exact laptop's bus/speed and compute capability (expected Ampere SM 8.6) and check kernel availability instead of inferring it from nominal precision. If the original laptop is unavailable, obtain approval for a named alternative physical 4 GiB device with its own measured performance and launch support. The reproducibility and results apply to that exact platform. [RTX 5080 specifications](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/geforce/graphics-cards/50-series/rtx-5080/) · [RTX 2050 example SKU](https://marketplace.nvidia.com/en-gb/consumer/gaming-laptops/asus-tuf-gaming-a15-fa506nf-gaming-laptop-amd-ryzen-5-7535hs-16gb-ddr5-512gb-ssd-nvidia-geforce-rtx-2050-4gb-windows/) · [Historical host](../experiments/DML-0001.md)

**Alternative and consequence.** A software-limited 5080 measures the big card's kernels and bandwidth under an accounting cap. It can screen byte fit. It cannot establish 2050 portability, physical residency, or a physical 4 GiB speed claim. Access, device overhead, shared-memory support, FP4 or fused code reconstruction and driver availability remain unknown.

## S2 · Complete memory and speed measurement

**Recommendation.** Require successful cold load, prefill, and full decode on the physical device under **4,294,967,296 bytes absolute device-wide peak** for three independent cold processes. Include device residency for weights, scales, indices, embeddings, learned tables/router, MTP, KV/state, activations, temporary reconstruction and staging, attention/GEMM workspace, allocator reservation and fragmentation, CUDA/driver and display reservation. Report idle and total absolute peaks. Keep idle headroom visible and never subtract it from the qualifying cap. Disable managed-memory spill, offload and external runtime model calls. Audit device/host transfer traces to separate ordinary input/output transfers from forbidden parameter/state movement.

Sample `nvmlDeviceGetMemoryInfo_v2.used` when available at a declared cadence, proposed 5–10 ms, and log timestamped phase boundaries and synchronized snapshots. Record allocator allocated and reserved high-water marks, reproducible allocation or driver telemetry and an independent physical completion check. Keep byte domains separate to avoid double-counting subprocesses or reserved/allocated capacity. Polling can miss transients. Document coverage, unavailable counters, whether instrumentation perturbs the peak, and guard-band smaller than available headroom before certification. On Windows WDDM, per-process NVML memory may be unavailable, which favors device-wide telemetry and a headless Linux comparison where possible. Repeat the memory and speed runs with the same driver, workload and powered operating point. [NVML](https://docs.nvidia.com/deploy/nvml-api/latest/api/group__nvmlDeviceQueries.html)

Predeclare a frozen battery of 8K inputs and at least 256 actual output tokens. Count tokens from the serialized final stream (including reasoning if enabled) when the engine delivers them. Calculate post-first-token decode as `(n−1)/(t_last−t_first)` using synchronized output timestamps. Each cold process must achieve at least eight generated native tokens/s on that workload, with p95 inter-token latency, UTF-8 bytes/s, prefill rate and time-to-first-token reported. A prematurely stopped sequence is a specified failure for the performance workload, and quality tests retain their own appropriate stop rules. Effective speed and GPU memory remain separate from capability.

**Alternative and consequence.** Allocator-only or per-process memory is useful during development and omits device costs. Deducing a physical peak from file bytes or periodically queried allocator counters is insufficient. The physical capacity and measured utilization on the target still need preflight.

## S3 · Reasoning and output budget

**Recommendation.** Use identical raw task text and a common prompt policy per task. On short tasks, cap the combined input and generation at 8,192 reference tokens and accommodate each model's native context window. Count wrappers, special tokens, exposed reasoning and final answer. Require engines to expose or otherwise bound any billed hidden reasoning. Select each model's strongest compliant reasoning and decoding setup on disjoint development items under the same search budget, then freeze it and the handling of truncated or unclosed answers. Compare those configurations as the quality-first primary condition. Report matched generation-token and measured-compute sensitivities plus an auditable direct-answer condition. Report native counts and raw UTF-8 sizes. The long-context suite counts full prompt length and at least 256 native output tokens separately.

**Alternative and consequence.** A vendor-published or unbounded reasoning profile is a separate upper-reference result. Equal tokens do not equal FLOPs or energy. Log actual forward applications, accepted/rejected work, latency and estimated FLOPs. Compare matched-compute or latency as a separate frontier when the counters are trustworthy. Prove tokenizer truncation and scratch accounting in a harness verification plan before accepting this budget.

## S4 · Comparable and usable 8K context

**Recommendation.** Freeze exact UTF-8 task content of 2K, 4K and 8K lengths measured by the pinned Qwen tokenizer. Publish bytes, Unicode boundaries, reference and native token counts, and chat-template overhead. Require processing without clipping for both 8,192 native prompt tokens and 8,192 common task-content reference tokens, with wrappers and at least 256 available native output tokens. Include evidence at the beginning, middle and end, distractors, aggregation, multi-hop RULER items and unseen natural documents with delayed evidence. The proposed acceptance criteria are simultaneous normalized ≥0.80 on 8K RULER under S7, no more than 10 percentage points of paired macro drop from 2K to 8K on matched task families, and a numerical natural delayed-evidence minimum frozen by the reviewer before scoring. Publish absolute RULER macro and family scores, including the earlier 80%/60% targets as diagnostics, plus both length sweeps. Freeze the statistical rule for the drop and natural endpoint with S6. [RULER](https://github.com/NVIDIA/RULER)

**Alternative and consequence.** Fixed UTF-8 bytes are a tokenizer-neutral reporting axis and may vary by script and content density. Native 8192-token claims alone are susceptible to segmentation differences. Select languages and fixed payloads before finalizing the exact length mapping and floor.

## S5 · Independent reference scores and scoring tools

**Recommendation.** Independently produce all six primary denominators. MMLU-Pro, MATH-500 and chosen 8K RULER are missing aligned Qwen public scores. Reproduce GPQA Diamond, LiveCodeBench and IFBench because publisher configurations differ or are incomplete. Reproduce all six for Gemma for a separate comparison. Archive per-item scores, judge version and execution tests, exact generation/stop parameters, sample IDs, prompt hashes, error policies, and reference score provenance. Use pinned EleutherAI adapters where appropriate, with official task packages for code execution and instruction/context scoring. For coding use a separate offline sandbox for submitted code grading and never let the tested model interact with it or call other tools. Avoid mixing score variants or cherry-picking a reference runtime. [Evaluation harness](https://github.com/EleutherAI/lm-evaluation-harness) · [MMLU-Pro](https://github.com/TIGER-AI-Lab/MMLU-Pro) · [GPQA](https://github.com/idavidrein/gpqa) · [MATH-500 data](https://huggingface.co/datasets/HuggingFaceH4/MATH-500) · [LiveCodeBench](https://github.com/LiveCodeBench/LiveCodeBench) · [IFBench](https://github.com/allenai/IFBench) · [RULER](https://github.com/NVIDIA/RULER)

**Alternative and consequence.** Publisher scores are good provenance for an unconstrained scoreboard and cannot populate missing or mismatched entries. Reference models or training mixtures can include public benchmarks. Freeze exact and approximate overlap checks against the student's and any teacher-output corpora. Use date-filtered coding items, novel procedurally generated items and fresh knowledge probes with publisher/teacher cutoff uncertainty declared. If no credible common clean overlap remains for a task, leave parity unresolved or approve a documented new release for both models. No reference inference or model download has happened in this phase.

The official LiveCodeBench `release_v6` covers May 2023 through April 2025. Its published scores are a historical diagnostic. A fresh primary coding track requires a separately pinned release or sealed authored items after the latest credible exposure cutoff across candidate, references and any teacher. A checkpoint revision date is not proof of its training cutoff. When the cutoff or valid item count cannot be established, report the historical track with contamination uncertainty and leave the clean primary denominator unresolved. [Official release windows](https://github.com/LiveCodeBench/LiveCodeBench#dataset)

## S6 · Paired statistics and qualification of the 95% claim

**Recommendation.** Use the existing six weights. For task category `j`, define `r_j=(p_dom,j−c_j)/(p_qwen,j−c_j)` and `DCI=Σ_j w_j r_j` without clamping. Use item-level option floors averaged over the frozen item sample where applicable, e.g. 0.25 for four-choice GPQA. Use 0 for generative tasks. Keep item prompts and option order paired. Use one deterministic prediction where applicable and a fixed eight samples per item for sampled tasks, averaged to an expected pass@1/accuracy. Estimate uncertainty by paired resampling of independent items/clusters within each task and joint model resampling of their corresponding outcomes. If observations share a template, source chapter or coding contest, bootstrap those blocks. Nest seed or generation sampling where necessary and report both item and evaluation-seed variation. For architecture-level conclusions use separately trained seeds, and give fixed-checkpoint qualifications their narrower interpretation.

Preregister a one-sided 95% lower confidence bound **≥0.95** for primary DCI and simultaneous one-sided category floors under S7. Use a simultaneous bootstrap or familywise correction across endpoints. Retain raw task intervals and denominator uncertainty. If a reference denominator is indistinguishable from chance, report the category ratio as unresolved and withhold composite qualification until the definition is revised and approved. A 198-item GPQA illustration gives about 2.2 points raw SE near 89%. Eight samples per question do not create 1,584 independent questions. Size any expansion by initial paired variance and frozen external examples with preplanned inclusion. No sequential peeking or margin changes.

**Alternative and consequence.** Mark a point estimate ≥0.95 with lower bound ≥0.90 as a promising result with unresolved parity. Either approach requires reporting missing outcomes, cluster structure and limited conclusions. Approval is needed for exact sampling and interval choices.

## S7 · Per-category floors

The following are proposed simultaneous normalized lower-bound floors for broad parity, with raw values also reported. The historical minimum of 0.60 remains a candidate progression floor for intermediate M4/M5 milestones. A chance-adjusted 0.80 floor is stricter for the 95% claim and limits compensation by one strong task.

| Category and DCI weight | Proposed benchmark and minimum |
|---|---|
| General knowledge and reasoning · 25% | MMLU-Pro accuracy, normalized ≥0.80. For broad factual capability also require normalized ≥0.80 on a sealed, unweighted long-tail short-answer guardrail against the same Qwen checkpoint. Treat abstentions as failures for the guardrail and report coverage/calibration by frequency and domain. An inconclusive guardrail leaves broad factual parity unresolved. |
| Scientific reasoning · 20% | GPQA Diamond accuracy, normalized ≥0.80. Record subject and option-order strata. |
| Mathematics · 15% | MATH-500 exact-answer accuracy, normalized ≥0.80. Keep GSM8K as a lower-scale diagnostic. |
| Coding · 20% | Date-filtered LiveCodeBench expected pass@1, normalized ≥0.80. Compile/runtime errors are failures. |
| Instruction following · 10% | IFBench official strict prompt success with a pinned metric variant, normalized ≥0.80. |
| Long-context handling · 10% | 8K RULER normalized ≥0.80, with the distance and natural delayed-evidence gates in S4. Report all three context lengths and raw family scores. |

**Alternative and consequence.** An aggregate-only threshold can conceal failed categories. Apply S6 simultaneous coverage to the six category floors and the separate factual guardrail. Higher absolute floors should be fixed using expected task reliability and qualifier definitions before score access. Contamination-audited fresh factual/coding subsets and exact per-category minimum item counts remain to be approved.
